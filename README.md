# 🏥 OptiMed

A professional platform to run your entire clinic from one place.

---

## 📌 Overview
OptiMed manages your whole clinic — appointment booking and queue organization, financial reports, patient records, and staff coordination for doctors and nurses — replacing scattered, manual workflows.

---

## ✨ Features
- 📅 **Pre-booking system** that organizes appointments and reduces crowding
- 🖥️ **Queue display screen** with professional call management
- 📊 **Financial reports** and performance analytics for the clinic and staff
- 💬 **Chat system** between doctors and nurses for faster communication
- 🗂️ **Complete patient profiles** (history – prescriptions – visits)
- 👩‍⚕️ **Dedicated nursing system** to organize in-clinic workflow
- 💊 **Full doctor system** with a database of 58,000+ medications
